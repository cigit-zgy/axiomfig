The following semantics are frozen for implementation:

1. `bar.simple` and `bar.grouped` support canonical external endpoint uncertainty:

   ```text
   simple:
   category | value | lower | upper

   grouped:
   category | group | value | lower | upper
   ```

   with `lower <= value <= upper`, finite/renderable geometry, and explicit `uncertainty_type`.

2. Existing `error` input remains executable compatibility. `error` and endpoint form are mutually exclusive. `lower` and `upper` are supplied together. The endpoint form is the recommended external CSV/DataFrame representation, especially for asymmetric intervals.

3. `bar.grouped` permits sparse `(category, group)` combinations. Missing row means absent/not supplied; explicit numeric zero means supplied zero. Missing data must never be silently filled with zero or aggregated.

4. Sparse grouped bars preserve first-seen category order and global first-seen group order. Global group slots stay semantically stable when a category lacks one group. Legends remain deterministic.

5. `bar.stacked`, `bar.normalized_stacked`, `bar.grouped_stacked`, `bar.diverging_stacked`, and `bar.mirrored` retain complete logical-grid requirements.

6. `bar.normalized_stacked` may perform its explicitly registered deterministic within-category plotting normalization when `normalization=normalize`. `normalization=proportion` validates supplied proportions. Other scientific/statistical/domain normalization remains upstream.

7. Figure Intent remains the sole Agent-to-runtime boundary and does not gain low-level backend visual parameters.
