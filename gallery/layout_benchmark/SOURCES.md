## Experimental helper scope

- `default`: Matplotlib/AxiomFig deterministic geometry only.
- `kiwi`: Kiwisolver 1.5.0, already available through Matplotlib, applies one shared constraint policy to grid rectangles. No case-specific Kiwi override is used.
- `adjusttext`: adjustText 1.4.0 applies one shared label-allocation policy only to IDs 08 and 09; all other geometry intentionally remains identical to `default`.
- `textalloc`: textalloc 1.2.4 applies one shared label-allocation policy only to IDs 08 and 09; all other geometry intentionally remains identical to `default`.

adjustText, textalloc, lifelines, and `img2pdf` were installed only in `tmp/layout-benchmark-venv` for benchmark/reference generation. None of these packages was added to AxiomFig's production dependencies.
